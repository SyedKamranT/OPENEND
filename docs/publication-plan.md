# Publication and review plan

**Status: PROVISIONAL, amended 2026-10-08.**

Publication readiness depends on a defensible question, complete methods, trustworthy evaluation, reproducible artifacts, and claims proportional to evidence. Positive results on H1?H5 are not a release requirement. Rigorous negative or inconclusive findings can be valuable.

The current manuscript is a proposal. The bounded policy-search pilot is insufficient to claim general algorithmic invention, a mature novelty verifier, superiority to major discovery systems, or open-ended scientific discovery.

Before a research submission:

1. Establish the precise contribution against close LLM-QD, heuristic-search, and novelty-assessment work.
2. Freeze a confirmatory protocol with justified task/seed counts, practical margins, independent novelty labels, and strong matched baselines.
3. Preserve all runs and produce tables from code. Document amendments and rerun affected comparisons when evaluator defects are found.
4. Conduct independent methodological review addressing optimization, agent systems, and novelty measurement. Label simulated reviews as simulated; they do not substitute for independent expertise.
5. Reproduce the artifact in a clean environment, distinguish API replay from fresh generation, and state remaining containment/data-access limits.
6. Resolve authorship, source licenses, participant protections where relevant, and release metadata.

Select a venue after the contribution is clear. Read its current official call for papers and policies then; dates in historical planning notes are not an authoritative schedule. No theorem or massive benchmark suite is treated as a universal venue requirement.
