\begin{abstract}
We study whether fixed-reference behavioral-distance parent weighting improves training-selected utility over otherwise matched uniform parent selection. The initial executable testbed searches a bounded expression language for online bin-packing policies. Both arms share generation, archive design, resource allowances, and evaluation; only parent-selection weights differ. Objective utility, behavioral diversity, and scientific novelty are treated as separate outcomes. The current reference screen cannot establish positive novelty and returns unresolved judgments for non-reference policies. Confirmatory experiments, a broader prior-art corpus, and independent mechanism-level adjudication remain future work. No superiority or validated-discovery claim is made in this proposal.
\end{abstract}
\section{Research Question}
Does fixed-reference behavioral-distance parent weighting improve the utility of training-selected policies over uniform parent selection at matched allowances? Independently validated discovery yield remains a broader, untested aspiration.
\section{Related Work}
Novelty search and MAP-Elites establish explicit exploration and quality-diversity objectives \citep{lehman2011abandoning,mouret2015illuminating}. ELM and LLMatic already combine language models with QD \citep{lehman2022elm,nasir2023llmatic}. FunSearch and AlphaEvolve maintain program diversity during executable search \citep{romera2024mathematical,alphaevolve2025}. EoH and BehaveSim are close comparators for heuristic design and behavioral similarity \citep{liu2024eoh,zhang2026behavesim}. Independent novelty measurement needs evidence-based evaluation \citep{novelty2025sdp,rqbench2026}.
\section{Pilot Method}
A common bounded JSON policy language, reference corpus, probe panel, and QD archive are used in both arms. Parent sampling is uniform or weighted by reference-relative behavioral sparsity. Full definitions and budgets are versioned in the repository protocol.
\section{Evaluation and Evidence Status}
Pilot outcomes must be generated from recorded artifacts. Any engineering results are descriptive and do not establish the primary novelty hypothesis. An unresolved novelty verdict is not converted into a positive label. Failed and incomplete comparisons are preserved.
\subsection{Development evidence as of 25 October 2026}
Phases 1--3 are closed for their documented development scope; baseline implementation
in Phase 4 remains open. A 240-attempt offline utility trial changed parents at 35 of
120 paired positions, but all runs selected the Best Fit fallback. A separate 640-attempt
trial across eight development blocks produced zero block-mean differences; this is
not equivalence evidence. A training-only expression used 1475 bins versus Best Fit's
1476, below the fixed 1\% screen (1461 bins), and was sensitive to algebraic rewriting
under floating-point execution. A fixed-parent, common-seed assay of 512 mutations
found no improvement over its best supplied parent; two proposals merely retained that
known gain. All these are development diagnostics, not confirmatory estimates.

Native baseline audits identify action-space, prompt and sampling-scale differences
from the JSON adaptations. The latest live baseline attempt is incomplete (31 starts,
30 saved responses); unknown usage and missing arms are preserved. Virtualization is
currently deferred, and generated Python containment has not been validated. These
limits preclude native-comparator superiority claims. Artifact pointers and prospective
protocols are maintained in the repository research log.
\section{Limitations}
The policy language excludes general code generation, the instance distributions are synthetic, and the reference set is small. Fixed-probe distances are not algorithmic-equivalence proofs. Hosted-model training contamination and stochasticity are not eliminated by retrieval restrictions or local seeds. Generalization and practical effect margins require further study.
\section{Research Integrity}
Negative and inconclusive findings are eligible for reporting. Claims will be revised to match evidence, with protocol amendments and failed runs retained.
